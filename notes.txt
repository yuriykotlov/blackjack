rules

 - beat the dealer:
 -> beat their hand, up to 21, or else it will be a bust.

 - cards:
 -> number on card = number
 -> jacks, queens, kings, count as 10.

 - aces:
 -> aces count as 1 or 11.
 -> ace prioritises being 11, but if it causes bust, ace = 1.

 - on init cards:
 -> if value of cards == 21, blackjack.


start:

 - players place bet.
 - players dealt cards.
 - player options: hit, stand, double down, split, fold.
 - only hit and stand available as option after the second dealing of cards. 
 -> hit: take another card
 -> stand: dont take any more cards.
 -> double down: double the bet and take one card.
